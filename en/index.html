Stone hammam ritual

Classic

A traditional hammam: warming on the stone, then scrub and foam.

scheduleTotal 60 minTRY 1,450

  1. Warm-up · 15 min — You lie on the warm stone and let your body warm up.
  2. Scrub · 15 min — Dead skin is gently removed with a single-use mitt.
  3. Foam · 15 min — A cloud of foam from olive-oil soap.
  4. Tea · 15 min — Linden or sage tea in the rest room.

Olive-oil massage ritual

A relaxing massage with warm olive oil, starting with a foot bath.

scheduleTotal 75 minTRY 1,900

  1. Feet · 10 min — Salt and lavender in warm water.
  2. Back and shoulders · 25 min — Back and shoulder massage with slow, long strokes.
  3. Full body · 30 min — Legs, arms and neck; you set the pressure.
  4. Tea · 10 min — Herbal tea in the quiet room.

Facial ritual

A calm facial with cleansing, steam and a mask.

scheduleTotal 50 minTRY 1,250

  1. Cleanse · 10 min — A gentle cleanser suited to your skin type.
  2. Steam · 10 min — Warm steam and a hot towel.
  3. Mask · 20 min — A clay or rose mask, with a hand massage meanwhile.
  4. Moisturise · 10 min — A light moisturiser and sunscreen.

Ritual for two

For two

Hammam and massage in the same room at the same time; the price is for two.

scheduleTotal 105 minTRY 3,900

  1. Hammam · 30 min — Warming up at a private basin.
  2. Scrub and foam · 20 min — Two attendants work at the same time.
  3. Massage · 40 min — Two massage tables side by side.
  4. Garden · 15 min — Tea and dried fruit under the olive trees.
